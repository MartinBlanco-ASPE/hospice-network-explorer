Three questions

General wording uses unweighted positive pointwise mutual information (PPMI) to show phrases appearing together above their marginal-frequency independence baseline. Potentially serious wording uses record weights of 1 + the provisional narrative-severity score and includes nine severity-family diamonds. Compare sources switches between two saved selections on one fixed position frame with a shared size scale; simultaneous side-by-side panels are deferred.

Size, connections, and colors answer different questions

Phrase-node area counts distinct identified hospices with matching wording. Connections in the main presets use within-record phrase presence. General-view community colors are fitted to the displayed connections using weighted greedy modularity; they are exploratory wording clusters, not the severity-word list or validated themes. Severity-family diamonds are predefined lexicon nodes and can appear in groups with other care phrases. Severity share remains provisional. IJ and condition level are separate source indicators. Group numbers and hues are local to a view.

Presets and Advanced

Main presets use evidence-oriented text, lift at least 1.5 (PPMI = log2(1.5)), at least 25 shared records for phrase links and 10 for severity-node links, and the union of each endpoint's four strongest connections. The Calendar year dropdown above Advanced supports one year, multiple years together, or All years. Selected years combine with source, context and severity filters and also apply to both source-comparison selections. Repeat appearances count distinct matching survey dates within the selected years; they do not establish new failures or recurrence rates. Advanced retains text sensitivities, diagnostics, TF-IDF, hospice incidence, condition-level colors, original record-community colors, and display controls. Overrides are marked custom. Reset restores the preset while retaining main source/context selections.

Source identities and interpretation

Guided views consistently normalize assigned CCN letter case, giving 6,486 distinct assigned CCNs in the candidate corpus; earlier outputs retain 6,487 raw keys. Pending identities remain in record views but not hospice counts. PPMI is not a significance test or an adjustment for survey selection, provider clustering, or all documentation practices. Broad aliases and quoted templates can influence links and stable groups. Source groups may include the same hospice and are not independent populations.

Reading coverage and validation

The phrase analysis covers 67,594 candidate care records. AI reading/coding decisions cover 96 selected narratives; the coding tab contains only the original 48. Human validation, codebook reconciliation, severity review, and a complete 2026 policy endpoint check remain pending. Threshold/resolution checks assess algorithmic stability, not clinical or thematic validity. No patient-harm prevalence, provider ties, policy effectiveness, or thematic saturation is inferred. Independent human validation remains pending.
